Extreme normal errors: what is verified?

The frozen targets and saved score calculation pass their checks. A valid model-side repair is still unresolved.

No benchmark score, ranking formula or selected recipe changes in this audit. A score matching its saved image does not prove that the model uses the right native coordinate convention or that its inference is numerically correct.

All 17 source images and targets reconstruct exactly from the original DIODE records. The angular scorer passes known 0°, 90° and 180° vectors and the frozen invalid-prediction rule. The 11 model recipes selected at the time of this audit had all 17 normal query scores replayed from saved images. Current table defaults may differ; this audit retains its original recipe labels and measurements. Across 16 checked conditions, 391 candidate images reproduce 272 reported query scores to floating-point precision. Analogist retains all eight candidates per query and their arithmetic mean.

Painter’s six black predictions are already collapsed in finite floating output before image conversion. They occur at queries 0, 5, 7, 12, 14 and 16; their demonstrations contain valid normals. No channel, dtype or scorer repair has been established.

Other saved failures: VICT has five fully black normal images (q0, q5, q7, q12 and q14); LaVin-DiT P2 has one (q3). Their published scores reproduce exactly. Unlike Painter’s retained floating-output check, these PNG checks do not locate the failure before or after image conversion.

PromptDiffusion’s paired range change: mean error increases from 83.8801° to 84.2375° (+0.3574°, 0.426% worse). Its mean black-pixel fraction rises from 0.233% to 2.220%. This is a descriptive breakdown of the unchanged metric, not a reason to discard invalid predictions.

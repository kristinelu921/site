What was checked

Original episodes and targets

All 17 original demonstrations and queries are retained. The class-ID recovery is unique and reproduces every demonstration mask exactly. Scoring inputs, targets and copy scores match across arms.

Released task adapter

The authenticated checkpoint runs in CPU FP32. The native palette, bilinear output conversion and L1 class decoder are checked against the released functions. All 34 model outputs are finite, and their saved images reproduce from their raw outputs.

Measured runtime

Both 17-query arms completed in 3.41 minutes on eight CPU threads, including 8.49 seconds loading the model. This is a CPU observation, not a TPU throughput measurement.
